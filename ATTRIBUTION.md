# Asset attribution

Every file asset in this repository (images, fonts, HDRIs, materials, sounds) needs a row here.

| File | Author | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/icon/` (app icon: SVG, PNG, ICNS, ICO) | ShapeCraft contributors | Original work, generated by `assets/icon/gen/` | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |
| `assets/home/` (home screen pictures: tutorial and What's new cards) | ShapeCraft contributors | Original work: our own models rendered with `shapecraft-cli run … --png --clear` by `assets/home/gen/make_art.py`, and screenshots of our own Fillet card and Navigation Presets window | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |
| `website/media/` (site recordings and stills) | ShapeCraft contributors | Original work: our own app recorded offscreen by `website/gen/make_gifs.sh`; the link preview card `og-card.jpg` from `website/gen/og-card.html` with our own render | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |
| `website/media/icon.svg` | ShapeCraft contributors | Copy of `assets/icon/shapecraft.svg` | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |
